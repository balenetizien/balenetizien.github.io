## Engine Specifications: 1.0T Turbo vs 1.2 NA

| Specification | 1.0T Turbo | 1.2 NA |
|--------------|-----------|--------|
| Engine | 1KR-VET, 3-cylinder turbo | 1NR-VE, 4-cylinder |
| Displacement | 996 cc | 1,197 cc |
| Max Power | 98 PS @ 6,000 rpm | 88 PS @ 6,000 rpm |
| Max Torque | 140 Nm @ 2,400–4,000 rpm | 113 Nm @ 4,200 rpm |
| Transmission | CVT | AT (4-speed) / CVT |
| Fuel Economy | 18.0 km/L (WLTC) | 16.1 km/L (WLTC) |
| Fuel Type | Pertamax (RON 92+) | Pertalite (RON 90) |

### 1.0T Turbo Advantages:
- Higher peak power and torque despite smaller displacement
- Excellent low-RPM torque (140 Nm from just 2,400 rpm) — great for city driving
- More responsive acceleration
- Better fuel economy despite higher performance

### 1.0T Turbo Disadvantages:
- Requires minimum RON 92 fuel (Pertamax) — slightly higher fuel cost
- 3-cylinder engines can feel slightly less smooth than 4-cylinder
- Turbo adds a component that can require maintenance

### 1.2 NA Advantages:
- Can use RON 90 (Pertalite) — cheaper fuel
- 4-cylinder is inherently smoother
- Lower long-term maintenance cost (no turbo)
- AT 4-speed is simpler and extremely reliable

### 1.2 NA Disadvantages:
- Lower peak performance
- Torque comes in higher in the RPM range — less responsive at low speeds
- Worse fuel economy than 1.0T

## Which Variant Should You Choose?

**Raize 1.2 G AT** — if you want the lowest purchase price and don't need high performance. Extremely reliable AT, cheap fuel (Pertalite). Best for light city driving or as a first car.

**Raize 1.0T G CVT** — the sweet spot. Turbocharged performance, CVT smoothness, competitive price. Best all-around choice for most buyers.

**Raize 1.0T GR Sport CVT** — if you want the best performance, sportiest looks, and complete features (360 camera, paddle shifters). Worth the premium if budget allows.
