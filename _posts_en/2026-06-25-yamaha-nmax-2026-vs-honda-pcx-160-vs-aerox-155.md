NMAX, PCX, or Aerox?

## NMAX 2026 — The Benchmark

The NMAX has been the maxi-scooter benchmark for years. The 2026 version adds connectivity and smart features, but the fundamentals remain the same.

### Why People Love the NMAX

**Ride comfort on long trips**: NMAX's suspension setup and seat cushioning make it genuinely comfortable for 1–2 hour rides. The 127 kg weight is well-distributed — it doesn't feel heavy at low speeds.

**Massive storage**: 26.5L under-seat — fits a full-face helmet and daily work bag simultaneously. One of the best storage volumes at this price point.

**Large fuel tank**: 7.1L tank + 47 km/L average = range of over 300 km on a full tank. Fewer fuel stops on daily commutes.

**Reliability record**: The NMAX has a long track record in Indonesia. Tens of thousands of units have been running for 3–5 years with minimal issues.

### NMAX 2026 Turbo Variant — Worth It?

The NMAX Turbo adds a genuine turbocharged version of the 155cc engine with significantly higher power output. Is it worth the IDR 4+ million premium over the standard? For most daily commuters: probably not. For enthusiasts who want genuine performance on the highway: yes.

## PCX 160 — The Practical Choice

The PCX 160 jumped from 150cc to 160cc in 2021 and hasn't looked back. It now leads the segment in outright performance numbers.

### Why the PCX 160 Stands Out

**Best power and torque in class**: 15.8 PS and 15 Nm — the highest in this comparison. This translates to more confident highway overtaking and easier uphill riding with a passenger.

**Largest fuel tank**: 8.1L tank means the longest range — approximately 390 km between fill-ups. Ideal for long-distance commuters.

**Largest storage**: 30L under-seat is class-leading — fits a full-face helmet with room to spare. If you carry a laptop bag daily, the PCX is hard to beat.

**e:HEV Hybrid**: The PCX 160 e:HEV adds a mild hybrid system that improves low-speed torque and fuel economy. Unique in this segment — no direct competitor offers this.

### PCX 160 Drawbacks

- **Heaviest in class**: 132 kg is notably heavier than NMAX (127 kg) and Aerox (118 kg). Felt most when maneuvering in tight spaces or parking.
- **Higher base price**: IDR 32 million for the CBS starts above Aerox S and close to NMAX Standard.

## Aerox 155 — The Sporty Outlier

The Aerox 155 plays a different game. Where NMAX and PCX prioritize comfort and practicality, Aerox targets riders who want a maxi-scooter with genuine sport DNA.

### Why the Aerox 155 Is Different

**Lightest in class**: 118 kg — 9–14 kg lighter than competitors. This makes a real difference in urban agility and quick direction changes.

**Most affordable entry point**: At IDR 28.5 million for the standard, it's IDR 3.5 million cheaper than the PCX 160 CBS. The savings can buy accessories or a good helmet.

**Sporty ergonomics**: The Aerox's low seat height (790 mm) and slightly more aggressive riding position suit taller riders and those who prefer a sportier feel.

### Aerox 155 Drawbacks

- **Smallest storage**: 14L is barely enough for a compact helmet. If you need to carry bags daily, you'll need a top box or rear bag.
- **Smallest fuel tank**: 5.7L means more frequent fuel stops — approximately 270 km range vs PCX's 390 km.
- **Not ideal for long trips**: The sportier ergonomics and firmer suspension make extended riding less comfortable than NMAX or PCX.

## Final Verdict

**Choose NMAX 2026** if: You want the most proven, most comfortable, most balanced maxi-scooter. The go-to choice for long daily commutes and occasional highway trips. The Turbo variant if you want genuine performance.

**Choose PCX 160** if: Storage capacity and maximum range are priorities, or you want the most powerful stock engine in the class. The e:HEV if fuel cost matters most.

**Choose Aerox 155** if: You want the sportiest riding experience, the most agile urban scooter, and the lowest entry price. Best for younger riders who value style and agility over pure practicality.

There is no wrong answer among these three. The best maxi-scooter is the one that fits your specific commute profile and budget.
