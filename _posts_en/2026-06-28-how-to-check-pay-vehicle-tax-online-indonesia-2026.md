Who enjoys queuing at Samsat? Nobody, I imagine. Arrive early in the morning, take a number, sit and wait for two hours, get called, turn out to have a missing document, come back tomorrow. A classic experience almost every vehicle owner in Indonesia has had.

The good news: over the past several years, the Indonesian government has been seriously building digital infrastructure for vehicle tax matters. And in 2026, the system is far more mature than before.

In this article, I'll explain the **complete guide to checking and paying vehicle tax online** — from which apps to use, clear step-by-step instructions, to things to watch out for so you don't fail payment or incur late fees. All based on current 2026 conditions.

## First, Let's Understand: What Types of Vehicle Tax Are There?

Before getting to the how, it's important to know what you're paying. This is what often causes confusion.

### 1. PKB (Pajak Kendaraan Bermotor / Motor Vehicle Tax)

This is the most common — paid **every year**. The amount is calculated from the vehicle's sale value, which is updated annually. The older the vehicle, the lower the sale value, and the lower the PKB.

This PKB is what's printed on your STNK (registration document), and what people mean when they say "pay annual car/motorcycle tax."

### 2. STNK Renewal (every 5 years)

Every 5 years, you must replace your license plate and physically renew your STNK — this cannot be done online because it requires a physical vehicle inspection. What CAN be done online is the regular annual PKB tax payment.

### 3. SWDKLLJ (Road Accident Fund Contribution)

This is usually included in the annual tax bill. The amount is fixed (doesn't depend on vehicle value) and is paid together with PKB.

### 4. Late Payment Penalty

If you pay PKB late, there's a penalty. The rate is 2% per month of the outstanding PKB amount, up to 48 months (96%). So if you're 2 years late, the penalty can almost equal the base tax — this is what often shocks people.

## Method 1: SIGNAL App (Most Recommended)

SIGNAL (Samsat Digital Nasional) is the official national app from the Korlantas Polri (Traffic Police). This is the most complete and reliable method for most Indonesian provinces.

### How to Download

- **Android**: Play Store → search "SIGNAL Samsat Digital"
- **iPhone**: App Store → search "SIGNAL Samsat Digital"

Make sure you download the official app — look for the Korlantas Polri logo.

### Step-by-Step Guide

**Step 1: Register an Account**
1. Open the SIGNAL app
2. Tap "Register"
3. Enter NIK (National ID number)
4. Upload a photo of your ID card (KTP)
5. Take a selfie (for face verification)
6. Enter your phone number and email
7. Wait for OTP verification

**Step 2: Add Your Vehicle**
1. In the main menu, tap "Add Vehicle"
2. Enter your license plate number
3. Enter your vehicle chassis number (found on STNK and physically stamped on the car/motorcycle)
4. The system will verify automatically

**Step 3: Check Tax Amount**
1. Select the vehicle you want to pay for
2. Tap "Annual Tax Payment"
3. Tax information and total amount due will appear — including PKB amount, SWDKLLJ, and any late penalty

**Step 4: Payment**
1. Select payment method (bank transfer, VA/Virtual Account, e-wallet)
2. Follow the payment instructions
3. After successful payment, download proof of payment

**Step 5: Download E-STNK**
After payment is confirmed, your electronic STNK is available for download in the app. This document is legally valid.

### Payment Methods Available in SIGNAL

## Method 3: Via the Bank's Mobile Banking App

Several major banks have integrated vehicle tax payment directly into their mobile banking:

### BCA Mobile
1. Open BCA Mobile
2. Select m-BCA → m-Payment
3. Select MPN G3 / Vehicle Tax
4. Enter license plate number
5. Pay with BCA balance

### BRI Mobile
1. Open BRImo
2. Select "Tax Payment"
3. Select "Vehicle Tax"
4. Enter license plate number and NIK
5. Pay with BRI balance

### Mandiri Mobile
1. Open Livin' by Mandiri
2. Select "Bill Payment"
3. Select "Samsat"
4. Enter vehicle data
5. Pay with Mandiri balance

## Things to Watch Out For: Common Failure Causes

### 1. Chassis Number Doesn't Match
The most common reason for failed verification. Ensure you enter the chassis number EXACTLY as it appears on your STNK and physical vehicle. Some numbers look similar — "I" (letter i) vs "1" (number one), "O" (letter o) vs "0" (number zero).

### 2. Tax Period Lapse (Over 1 Year Late)
If your tax has been expired for over 1 year, you generally cannot use the online system and must go to Samsat in person to process the penalty payment.

### 3. Different Owner's Identity
If the vehicle is still registered under a different person's name (previous owner), you may not be able to use the SIGNAL app. You need to transfer the vehicle name first.

### 4. Server Downtime
Especially at month-end and around Eid holidays. If the app fails to respond, try again after a few hours.

## Step-by-Step: Physical Samsat (When Online Doesn't Work)

For situations that must be done in person:

1. Bring: STNK (original), KTP (original, same owner as STNK), BPKB (for 5-year renewal)
2. Take a queue number at the Samsat of the vehicle's registration province
3. Inform the officer of what service you need
4. Wait to be called
5. Pay at the counter
6. Receive validated STNK

**Tips for easier in-person Samsat:**
- Arrive before opening time (usually 8:00 AM)
- Check your nearest Samsat's service hours online first
- Some Samsats have drive-through service for annual tax — much faster

## Conclusion

Paying vehicle tax online in 2026 has never been easier. The SIGNAL app covers the vast majority of Indonesian provinces and supports most major payment methods.

Key takeaway: don't wait until the tax expiry date. With online payment available 24/7, there's no longer a good excuse for paying late and incurring avoidable penalties.

Set a calendar reminder 1 month before your STNK expiry date. Then pay via SIGNAL from the comfort of your home. Done.
